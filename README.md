# landing-gear-solidworks
Conceptual design, 3D modeling, and assembly of a nose landing gear in SolidWorks.

Conceptual design and modeling project in SolidWorks focused on the development of a nose landing gear for aeronautical applications. The work includes the detailed design of individual parts, the complete assembly of the mechanism, and the generation of standardized technical drawings.

Author: Mateo Costa

Main assembly components:
- Landing gear leg (AISI 4340 Steel)
- Main landing gear support bracket (AISI 4340 Steel)
- Shock absorber body (Ti-6Al-4V Titanium alloy)
- Lower shock base (AISI 4130 Steel)
- Lower fork (AISI 1045 Steel)
- Helical spring (ASTM A1045 Steel)
- Wheel axle and hub (AISI 4340 and AISI 4130 Steel)
- Aircraft tire (Neoprene)
- Upper and lower torque links
- Locking rings and standardized hardware
- Hydraulic actuator and link arm

The project was developed through a documentary study of real configurations and advanced parametric modeling to simulate the movements of the mechanism.
